🛡️ Financial Safety

Protect Your Money Online

Learn how to identify common financial scams and protect your personal and banking information.

COMMON SCAMS

Know the Warning Signs

Understanding common scams can help you recognize suspicious situations.

📱

UPI & Payment Scams

Scammers may use fake payment requests, QR codes or misleading messages.

🔐

OTP & PIN Fraud

Never share your OTP, PIN or password with someone who contacts you unexpectedly.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, SMS messages and websites may try to steal your information.

💳

Fake Loan Apps

Be careful with unknown apps promising instant loans or unusually easy approval.

📈

Investment Scams

Be cautious of promises of guaranteed high returns with little or no risk.

👤

Impersonation Scams

Someone may pretend to be a bank employee, friend, company representative or official.

SAFETY CHECKLIST

Simple Rules to Remember

✓

Never share your OTP, PIN or password.

✓

Verify payment requests before sending money.

✓

Avoid clicking suspicious links.

✓

Use official websites and apps for financial services.

✓

Do not install unknown apps at someone's request.

✓

Be cautious when someone creates urgency or pressure.

🚨 If You Suspect a Financial Fraud

Act quickly. Contact your bank or payment service through its official channel and preserve relevant transaction details, messages and screenshots.

In India, financial cyber-fraud can also be reported through the official government cybercrime reporting channels.
